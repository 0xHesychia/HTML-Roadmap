The Ultimate Chocolate Chip Cookies

Baking the perfect cookie requires precision, patience, and love.

Key Ingredients

  • Unsalted butter (softened)
  • Brown sugar and white sugar
  • Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Baking Steps

  1. Cream the butter and sugars together until fluffy.
  2. Mix in the eggs and vanilla extract.
  3. Slowly add dry ingredients, then fold in chocolate chips.

Baking Terminology

Creaming
To beat ingredients together until they form a smooth, uniform paste. Folding
Folding
To gently combine a light mixture with a heavier mixture without losing air.
